# Discovery interview script (use on calls, 20 min)

Goal: verify pain, current workaround, willingness to pay. Do not pitch before question 6. Record answers verbatim in VALIDATION_LOG.md with date and company.

1. How many client sites do you look after right now, and what's the stack mix?
2. Which of those are on a monthly care/maintenance retainer, and what does the retainer include?
3. Walk me through the last time you reported that work to a client. What exactly did you send, and how long did it take?
4. What tools do you use today for that reporting? What do you like, what annoys you?
5. Has a client ever questioned what the retainer pays for? What happened?
6. (Only if pain is confirmed) I am building CareProof: you add the site URLs, it runs checks (uptime, SSL, security headers, links, domain expiry) and generates a branded monthly report, any stack, no plugin. Here is a 90-second sample. (Show sample report.)
7. If it saved you the monthly compile time, what would that be worth per month?
8. Would you take a 2-month pilot at 50% off (USD 9.50-24.50/mo by plan size), cancel anytime?
9. If yes: who else in your position should I talk to?

Qualification rule: counts as a qualified conversation only if Q1 answer >= 10 sites AND Q3 answer includes manual assembly or per-tool exports.
