## What this changes about our claims

Earlier landing copy said incumbents "lock white-label behind enterprise tiers". That is NOT defensible: MainWP includes white-label reports at $29/mo and WP Umbrella includes them at its base per-site price (both verified 2026-10-24). Corrected claims:

- TRUE and defensible: ManageWP and MainWP are WordPress-only; WP Umbrella is WordPress-only. None of them audit a mixed portfolio (Webflow, Shopify, custom) in one report.
- TRUE: uptime tools (UptimeRobot, Pulsetic) sell monitoring dashboards and status pages, not a branded client-facing care document; UptimeRobot gates white-label to $35/mo and Databox charges +$80/mo for branding.
- TRUE: per-site pricing (ManageWP add-ons, WP Umbrella ~$2.19/site) scales linearly with portfolio size; CareProof is flat by tier.
- TRUE: no verified competitor bundles this technical breadth (TLS expiry, HTTPS redirect, security headers, robots/sitemap, favicon, sampled links, RDAP domain expiry, TTFB/weight) into one client-facing report for any stack.

POSITIONING (updated on the landing page this session): lead with "mixed-stack technical audit in a branded monthly report", not with "white-label is rare". Within WordPress-only agencies the reporting ground is well covered (WP Umbrella, MainWP); our wedge is agencies whose portfolios are NOT all WordPress.

## Why an agency might stay with an incumbent (counter-evidence, honest list)

- WP-only agency with ManageWP/WP Umbrella already gets reports bundled with the maintenance execution (updates, backups) we do not do.
- MainWP lifetime pricing appeals to cost-sensitive self-hosters.
- UptimeRobot/Pulsetic cover real-time alerting (1-min intervals) we do not; we are a monthly proof-of-care layer, not an ops pager.
- Agencies that want PDFs emailed automatically get that from incumbents today (we do print-to-PDF + share link).
