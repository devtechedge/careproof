# Experiments

One experiment per entry: hypothesis, setup, metric, result, decision. No retro-filling of results.

## E1 - Report-in-minutes beats manual compilation (status: NOT STARTED, needs Dev approval to send)

- Hypothesis: web agencies on care retainers compile client reports manually at least monthly and will commit to a paid pilot for a branded any-stack report tool.
- Setup: 15 personalized outreach emails to agencies found in public directories; each email names one specific pain (manual monthly reporting) and offers a 2-month 50% pilot.
- Metric: qualified conversations / outreach sent; pilot commitments.
- Success: >= 2 pilot commitments within 7 days of first send.
- Result: (pending)

## E2 - Sample report as lead magnet (status: NOT STARTED)

- Hypothesis: a public sample report link converts landing visitors to waitlist signups with intake answers at >= 5%.
- Setup: landing page with sample report + intake form (sites managed, stack mix, current reporting method).
- Metric: waitlist intakes / unique landing visitors.
- Result: (pending)

## E3 - Community thread answer (status: NOT STARTED)

- Hypothesis: a genuinely useful answer in a recurring "which maintenance tool" Reddit thread generates >= 3 qualified visits to the landing page.
- Setup: one answer, Dev's account, full text approved by Dev first.
- Metric: referred visits and replies.

## E4 - Pricing sensitivity (status: NOT STARTED)
