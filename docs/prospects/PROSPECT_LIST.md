## Market pattern note (2026-10-24)

Entry care-plan pricing clusters at GBP 30-89 / $89-99 per month, 3 tiers is the norm; Webflow dev retainers $1.5k-5k/mo; Shopify care $299-399/mo. Only ~5 of 13 prospects publicly promise any monthly reporting, and those are one-line promises. Implication for positioning: the pain is "no proof" or "hand-made proof", not "no reporting tool awareness" - outreach should lead with the sample report, not with the category.
